Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

1. Introduction

Blue Heaven Restaurant & Bar respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how information submitted through this website may be used when you contact us or request a reservation.

2. Information You Provide

If you use the reservation form, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Reservation date
  • Reservation time
  • Number of guests

3. How We Use Reservation Information

Information provided through the reservation form is used to help process your reservation request, communicate with you and coordinate your visit to Blue Heaven Restaurant & Bar.

4. WhatsApp Reservations

When you submit the reservation form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a prepared message containing the details you entered. You must review and send that message yourself.

WhatsApp is a third-party service and its own privacy policy and terms apply.

5. Google Maps

The website may provide a link to Google Maps to help visitors find the restaurant. Google Maps is a third-party service and its own policies apply when you use it.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The current website is designed primarily as a static website and does not require users to create an account. Cookies or technologies used by embedded or third-party services may be governed by those services' policies.

7. Third-Party Services

The website may use external services for maps, fonts, image delivery or communication. These services may process information according to their own policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to handle information responsibly. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Reservation and contact information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for restaurant operations, customer service, record keeping or other legitimate business purposes.

10. Your Choices

If you have questions about information you have provided to Blue Heaven Restaurant & Bar, you may contact the restaurant and request clarification regarding how that information is being handled.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact Blue Heaven Restaurant & Bar, Goa.
